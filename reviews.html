Redmi Budget Series: Value & Performance Analysis

Published October 2026 | By Geiger

Redmi 15



The Xiaomi Redmi 15 is an entry-level budget friendly smartphone from the Xiaomi Redmi Series. It was introduced in February 2025 with availability rolling out from different countries around the world rather than it being released everywhere on the same day.


The following are the days it was released in different parts of the world:

  • Middle East & Africa: 1st August 2025.
  • Asia: 19th December 2025.
  • Europe: 5th August 2025.
  • South & North America: 1st August 2025.

One of the most important selling points about the Redmi 15 is the powerful 7000mah Battery that can last users for 2 days straight without charging. It also includes a 33W Fast charger which can make charging this phone very rapid and ready for use again as soon as possible.


Redmi 15 specifications:

  • 7000MAH Battery.
  • 33W Fast Charging.
  • 18W Reverse Charging.
  • Qualcomm Snapdragon 685 Processor (Fast & Effective for carrying out daily task easily).
  • Android 15 HyperOS with 2 years of System Updates.
  • Storage Options (128GB & 256GB).
  • RAM Options (4GB, 6GB, 8GB).
  • Network Options (4G & 5G LTE).
  • Refresh rate (144HZ).
  • Display (6.9 inches).

As seen above, the Redmi 15 shows quality power for easy use for day to day activites and it charges at a very good speed for a budget friendly smartphone. The starting price here in Nigeria is N148,000 for the base version (4GB + 128GB).


Rating: 8.5/10


Redmi 17



The Xiaomi Redmi 17 is a newer budget smartphone that was launched on 7th August 2026 globally with it having two variants just like the Redmi 15 (4G & 5G LTE).


The following are the days it was released around the world:

  • Africa: 8th August 2026.
  • Asia, Europe, North & South America: 6th August 2026 (Global).

One of the most important selling points of this phone is the slightly better battery capacity than that of the Redmi 15 (7500MAH) which makes using it better. It also comes with a massive 45W turbo charging which makes the charging experience faster.


Redmi 17 specifications:

  • 7500MAH Battery.
  • 45W Fast Charging.
  • 22W Reverse Charging.
  • MediaTek Helio G91-Ultra (Weaker processor than the Redmi 15).
  • Android 16 HyperOS with 2-3 years of System updates.
  • Storage Options (128GB & 256GB).
  • RAM Options (4GB, 6GB).
  • Network Options (4G).
  • Refresh Rate (120HZ).
  • Display (6.9 inches).

As seen above the Redmi 17 does not actually look like a step forward than that of the Redmi 15. If anything else, it looks more like a downgrade from the predecessor.


GeigerTech Verdict: The only positives that can make the Redmi 17 better than that of the Redmi 15 is the bigger battery capacity, faster charging speed and the Reverse charging speed. Other than those specifications, the phone to me looks like a massive downgrade.


The starting price for the Redmi 17 in Nigeria is N212,600.


Rating: 6/10
