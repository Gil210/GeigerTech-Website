AI & Automation: Will Artificial Intelligence Replace Humans?

Published November 2026 | By Geiger

The AI & Automation Debate



Artificial Intelligence has rapidly evolved from simple automation scripts to complex machine learning models capable of writing code, generating art, and analyzing complex datasets in real time.


Key Areas Impacted by Automation


  • Software Engineering: Automated code generation and automated testing pipelines.
  • Content & Design: Real-time image generation and automated copy editing.
  • Data Analysis: High-speed pattern recognition and financial forecasting.

The Debate

The news and information coming from around the world now is that AI is bound to replace human intervention whether we humans like it or not but I see this as an invalid statement because AI can never be us humans and it was intelligent minds (humans) that made it for the purpose of making research and getting complex tasks done easier.


I've been fortunate to use AI for the past 4 years now and I still experience faults in popular AI websites or apps like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude AI & many more. I can give AI a simple tasks and if I'm not able to read and understand what the AI has given me or just accept the response as just valid, the likelihood of having more errors in my day to day work and activites is high because AI has proven itself that it's capable of making mistakes just like us humans.


GeigerTech Verdict: AI has it's positives and negatives as we've seen today in the modern world. But as years keep flying by, AI will continue to be developed as an aid for engineers, workers and students to help get tasks done quickly.
