Who We Are


GeigerTech is an independent technology review platform dedicated to delivering transparent, data-backed hardware reviews, software analysis, and tech insights without market bias.


Our Core Focus Areas


  • Budget Hardware Evaluation: Testing smartphone battery longevity, real-world chip performance, and local market value pricing.
  • Software & OS Breakdown: In-depth look at major operating system upgrades, security features, and overall UI performance.
  • Tech & AI Trends: Objective evaluations of how emerging automation tools affect modern software engineering and workflow productivity.

Our Standards


We believe in testing devices against real everyday workloads—evaluating thermal performance, true battery cycles, and value for money in regional markets.
